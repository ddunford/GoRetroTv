# Listings provenance

`default.json` and `1998-12-24.json` carry the 68 individually named television services in the
Sky Digital launch line-up. Channel names and EPG numbers come from the launch table in
[TV Channel Lists](https://www.tvchannellists.com/w/List_of_line-up_changes_on_Sky_(UK_and_Ireland)_from_1982_to_1999).
Sky's contemporary corporate record says the platform launched with 140 channels; that larger
total includes grouped Sky Box Office services, Music Choice audio services, regional variants,
information services and adult services which are not individually named in the launch table.
Radio is outside this project's scope, and this guide does not invent identities or numbers for the
remaining grouped services.

The six schedules with named programmes are the reconstructed demo listings already present in this
repository. Every other launch service has one honest `Listings not yet reconstructed` entry in each
six-hour title-table block. This makes the real line-up and its firmware grouping available without
claiming programme data that has not been recovered from period listings. The later Listings phase
owns that reconstruction.
